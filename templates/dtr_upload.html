Commit Master DTR CSV

Super Admin only. These committed records are the official attendance source used by the Payroll Clerk for payroll generation.

Upload Official DTR

Upload the official attendance CSV file. Each upload will be saved as a separate DTR batch that the Payroll Clerk can select later.

Expected columns:

employee_no, work_date, time_in, time_out
Example: 
employee_no,work_date,time_in,time_out
2023106596,2026-10-01,08:00,17:00
2023106597,2026-10-01,08:15,17:05
2023106598,2026-10-01,08:30,
Important: The employee number in the CSV must match an existing Employee Master Record.

If an employee cannot be found, that row will be skipped.

DTR Workflow

Process:

Super Admin uploads the Master DTR
↓
System validates and saves the attendance records
↓
A unique DTR batch is created
↓
Payroll Clerk selects the DTR batch
↓
Payroll Clerk generates payroll
↓
Super Admin reviews and approves payroll
